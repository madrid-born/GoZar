This design pack shows the proposed Go Zar experience, from choosing important places to learning, remembering and exploring. Start with the clickable prototype; the English notes beside each Persian screen explain the workflow.

01 / Try the experience →A clickable 24-screen prototype. No installation, account or server needed.02 / Browse every screen →Individual mobile previews. Click a screen to open it in the prototype.03 / Understand the journey →A diagram connecting onboarding, lessons, reviews and exploration.04 / Explore the design system →Colors, type, controls and visual rules for later implementation.
Editable HTML/CSS source, PNG/JPG exports and engineering notes are included in the folders. Maps and data are illustrative. This is a design concept, not the working MAUI application.
